Fuel Type Classification
CFFDRS FBP System fuel types derived from 2025 City of Edmonton vegetation LiDAR

Classification Method

Individual tree crowns were classified as conifer or deciduous using a Random Forest classifier (v2) trained on the 2025 leaf-on LiDAR collection, achieving 90.4% accuracy.

Fuel types were assigned within UPLVI Vegetated and Naturalized Area polygons (Edmonton Open Data) at 20m × 20m resolution in EPSG:3776 (NAD83 / Alberta 3TM 114W).

  • ≥75% conifer trees → C-2 (Boreal Spruce)
  • ≥75% deciduous trees → D-2 (Green Aspen)
  • 25–75% conifer → M-2 (Boreal Mixedwood Green) + PC%
  • <2 trees per cell → O-1b (Standing Grass)
  • Outside natural areas → NF (Non-fuel)

Burn Probability Simulation
Monte Carlo fire simulation comparing two fire weather scenarios

Two Scenarios: Standard vs. High HFI

Two fire weather scenarios were modelled to bracket the range of wildfire risk across Edmonton's natural areas:

ScenarioDescriptionFire Weather Conditions
Standard Represents typical summer fire weather using mean seasonal FWI values. Most ignitions self-extinguish or remain small. Provides a baseline probability of fire occurrence under normal conditions. Mean seasonal FFMC, ISI, and BUI values from Edmonton-area weather stations
High HFI Summer Represents peak fire danger using elevated Head Fire Intensity conditions (90th+ percentile fire weather). Fires spread rapidly through grass and into forest fuels along the river valley, producing the yellow/orange/red corridors visible on the map. 90th percentile+ FFMC, ISI, and BUI values reflecting drought and high-wind conditions

Toggle between scenarios using the layer control on the map. The contrast between them illustrates how sensitive Edmonton's urban-wildland interface is to fire weather escalation.

Technical Notes

Data Sources

  • LiDAR: 2025 City of Edmonton Vegetation LiDAR (leaf-on)
  • Natural Areas: UPLVI Vegetated + Naturalized Areas (Edmonton Open Data)
  • Classifier: Random Forest v2 conifer/deciduous (90.4% accuracy)
  • Fire Model: CFFDRS FBP System / BurnP3 framework
  • CRS: EPSG:3776 (NAD83 / Alberta 3TM ref merid 114 W)
  • Resolution: 20m × 20m grid cells

Assumptions & Limitations

  • Leaf-on collection: D-2 and M-2 (green variants) used; for leaf-off modeling, substitute D-1/M-1
  • Spruce is Edmonton's dominant conifer; C-2 used over C-3
  • Grass classification based on UPLVI/naturalized boundaries, not spectral analysis
  • Urban canopy trees outside designated natural areas classified as NF (non-fuel)
  • Percent conifer raster provided for M-2 cells (BurnP3 PC input)
  • Two scenarios shown: High HFI (summer peak) and Standard conditions
